ICSP Walkthrough

Creating, filling out, activating and printing a student's Individual Crisis Support Plan. Written for social workers.

What the ICSP does in PowerSchool

An ICSP is two pieces that live together on the student's Student Support tab. Both have to exist for the plan to count.

Piece 1

The Plan

The blue card. It is the wrapper that says a plan exists, when it runs, and who owns it.

  • Type: Individual Crisis Support Plan (ICSP)
  • Start date and expiration date
  • Case Manager
  • Active or Inactive
Piece 2

The Form

The content: triggers, escalation behaviors, what de-escalates, restraint considerations, and the step-by-step crisis plan.

  • Opened from the link next to the plan card
  • Saved as a draft with Save for Later
  • Finished with Submit
Result

Where it shows up

Once the plan is active and the form is submitted, the ICSP appears in three places under Summit Tools.

  • ICSP list: every student, status, end date, case manager
  • ICSP Renewal Dashboard: upcoming expirations
  • Print / PDF (clean): one student or a whole caseload, for cottage staff
create first ↓then write ↓then it appears ↓
The most common mistake: filling out the form without ever creating the plan, or creating the plan and leaving the form as a draft. The list under Summit Tools only shows plans that have both.
Step 1 of 6

Open the student

Everything ICSP-related is done from the student's own record. Start there.

  1. 1
    Sign in to PowerSchool at summitnyack.powerschool.com/admin.
  2. 2
    Check the school selector in the top right says Summit School at Nyack, not District Office. Lists come up empty under District Office.
  3. 3
    Type the student's last name in the Search box at the top and press Enter, or click Student Search on the Start Page.
  4. 4
    Click the student's name. Their record opens with the photo and header strip at the top.
You can also get here from Summit Tools › ICSP: click the student's name in the left column of that list.

Step 2 of 6

Go to Forms, then the Student Support tab

The ICSP is not under Health or Behavior. It lives in Forms, on its own tab.

  1. 1
    In the student's left menu, open Student Profile and click Forms.
  2. 2
    The Forms page opens with three tabs: General Forms, Class Forms, Student Support. Click Student Support.
  3. 3
    Look at the left side of the page. If there is a blue card, this student already has a plan. If the left side is empty, there is no plan yet (go to Step 3).

Step 3 of 6

Create the plan (this is what activates it)

Saving a new plan makes the blue card appear with an Active badge. There is no separate "activate" button; the plan is active from the day you save it until its expiration date, or until someone deactivates it.

  1. 1
    On the Student Support tab, click the green New button.
  2. 2
    In the Add a New Plan box, set Type to Individual Crisis Support Plan (ICSP).
  3. 3
    Start Date: today, or the date the plan was reviewed with the team.
  4. 4
    Expiration Date: the review date. This drives the ICSP Renewal Dashboard, so set it to when the plan actually needs renewing (Summit plans run about six months).
  5. 5
    Click Save changes. The blue card appears on the left with the Active badge and your name as Case Manager.
Create the plan under your own login. PowerSchool records the person who saves the plan as the Case Manager. If someone else creates it for you, their name shows on the ICSP list, not yours.
Already has an old plan? Use Edit on the existing card to change the dates instead of making a second plan. Only make a New plan when the old one has been deactivated and you are starting over.

Step 4 of 6

Fill out the form

The form is the actual plan content. It has nine questions. Two are required, marked with a red asterisk.

  1. 1
    Still on Student Support, click the blue link on the right: Individual Crisis Support Plan (ICSP).
  2. 2
    Give the page a few seconds. The form loads after the header and can look blank at first.
  3. 3
    Answer each question. Put one item per line in the text boxes; the printed version keeps your line breaks.
  4. 4
    If you have to stop partway, click Save for Later. Your draft is kept, but the plan does not count as complete yet.

The questions, in order

  1. 1
    Program * Residential or Day
  2. 2
    Triggers that invoke potentially concerning behaviors
  3. 3
    Behaviors exhibited as crisis escalates, in order of appearance
  4. 4
    What has worked in the past to de-escalate the student

  6. 6
    Details of physical or emotional considerations regarding restraint
  7. 7

    Crisis Support Plan * steps in order, numbered
  9. 9
    Completed by (names and roles)

Step 5 of 6

Submit the form

Submit is the step that turns your answers into the live plan. Until you click it, the Summit Tools pages have nothing to show.

  1. 1
    Scroll to the bottom of the form and click the blue Submit button.
  2. 2
    If PowerSchool stops you, a required field is empty: Program or Crisis Support Plan. Fill it and submit again.
  3. 3
    Click < Student Forms at the top to return to the Student Support tab. The Last Entry time next to the form link updates to right now.

Save for Later vs Submit

  • D
    Save for Later keeps a draft on the form. The plan card can say Active while the content is still unfinished, and the ICSP list and print page show the old answers (or none).
  • ✓
    Submit files the answers as the student's current ICSP. Every later Submit replaces the previous one; the newest submission is what the list, the viewer and the print page use.
If you click away from the form, the browser asks "Leave site? Changes you made may not be saved." Choose Cancel to stay and submit. Choosing Leave throws away anything you typed since the last save.

Step 6 of 6

Check it, print it, renew it

The Summit Tools pages read straight from what you just submitted. Use them to confirm the plan took, to print for cottage staff, and to see what is coming due.

Confirm it took

  1. 1
    In the admin left menu, open Summit Tools (first item at the top) and click ICSP.
  2. 2
    Find the student. The chip should read ACTIVE · through (expiration date) · (your name). Tick Missing ICSP to see only students without one.

Print or email it

  1. 3
    Click the student's chip. The readable view opens.
  2. 4
    Click the teal Print / PDF (clean) button. In the print window choose Save as PDF to email it to the cottage, or print to paper. Print a whole caseload » prints every student on your list, one per page.

Update or renew

  1. 5
    Change the content: open the form from Student Support, edit, Submit. No need to touch the plan card.
  2. 6
    Renew: on the plan card click Edit, push the expiration date out, Save. The Renewal Dashboard clears.
  3. 7
    End it: click Deactivate this Plan on the card. The ICSP list then shows the student as INACTIVE. Avoid the red trash button; that deletes the plan and its history.

Common problems

The things that come up most. Each one has a one-line fix.

I filled out the form, but the student still shows "No ICSP" on the Summit Tools list.

One of two things. Either the form was saved with Save for Later instead of Submit, or no plan card was ever created on the Student Support tab. Go back to Student Support: if the left side is empty, click New and create the plan (Step 3), then open the form and click Submit (Step 5).

The student shows INACTIVE.

Either the expiration date has passed or someone clicked Deactivate. Open Student Support, click Edit on the plan card, set a new expiration date and save. If the card is gone, create a new plan.

The Case Manager is someone else's name.

The person who saved the plan card is recorded as Case Manager. If an admin created it on your behalf, ask Jon to switch it. Going forward, create the plan from your own login.

The form page is blank.

Wait a few seconds; the form loads after the page header. If it is still blank after ten seconds, reload the page (F5 or ⌘R).

The ICSP list shows zero students, or the wrong ones.

Check the school selector in the top right of PowerSchool. It should say Summit School at Nyack. District Office shows an empty list.

I need to change the plan content. Do I create a new plan?

No. Keep the plan card and open the form link, change the answers and Submit. The newest submission replaces the old one everywhere. Only use New when the previous plan has been deactivated and you are starting fresh.

The browser says "Leave site? Changes you made may not be saved."

You have unsaved answers on the form. Click Cancel to stay, then Submit. Leave discards the typing since your last save.

Printing cuts off the text boxes.

Use the teal Print / PDF (clean) button on the ICSP view page, not the browser's own print menu or the PowerSchool printer icon. The clean page lays the plan out for letter paper and hides empty sections.

Can cottage staff see this in PowerSchool?

No, cottage staff do not have PowerSchool logins. Save the clean print as a PDF and email it to them, or print a copy for the cottage binder.
